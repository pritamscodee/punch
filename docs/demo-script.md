# PUNCH: 3-minute demo script

Record on a real Android device (Seeker if available) against devnet. Screen-record with the device's recorder;
voice-over in one take. Prep: keeper running, wallet funded via **Settings → Get test kit**, second wallet already a
member of `DEMO` that will *miss* a shift (so a settlement appears), and you joined `DEMO` at least one shift ago.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Clock tab, unpaid: hazard band crawling, countdown | "Every habit app nags. PUNCH makes your streak expensive. This is a time clock: I have a bond in SKR, and this shift is unpaid." |
| 0:15 | Point at gauge, rail, band | "The gauge is my run. The band says the shift closes in four minutes and five SKR is at risk." |
| 0:25 | Tap **PUNCH IN** → Seed Vault / wallet sheet → approve | "One tap. Mobile Wallet Adapter, one signature, a memo record on Solana." |
| 0:40 | Key stamps PUNCHED, band turns flat enamel, needle sweeps | "Punched. The band clears, the run goes up. That took under ten seconds." |
| 0:50 | Floor tab: roster, one member OUT, "on the table" line | "This is the floor. Someone hasn't punched. When the shift closes, their penalty is split between everyone who did. No treasury, no house cut." |
| 1:10 | Scroll to SEEKER mark, QR invite, Share | "Seeker owners are verified with the Seeker Genesis Token. Bring a friend in by QR or link." |
| 1:25 | Ledger tab: figures, barcode, a *Settled · paid you* row → tap → Explorer | "Here's a closed shift. The keeper pulled the no-show's penalty through their SPL approval and paid me. Every row opens on Explorer." |
| 1:50 | Pact page for `DAWN`: terms, settler shield, "How the bond works" | "The bond never leaves your wallet. It's an SPL delegate approval: the settler can take one penalty per missed shift, nothing more. Unknown settlers get a warning." |
| 2:15 | New pact: name "5AM RUN CLUB", 24 H, 10 per miss → Open pact | "Start your own: one signature defines the pact on chain, registers it and bonds you." |
| 2:35 | Notification shade: shift-closing reminder | "And it reminds you before a shift closes, because the point is coming back every day." |
| 2:45 | Back on Clock | "PUNCH: clock in, or pay the ones who did. Built on Solana Mobile, bonded in SKR." |

Shots to avoid: long wallet loading spinners (cut them), devnet faucet steps (do them before recording).
